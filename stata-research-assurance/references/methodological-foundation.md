# Methodological Foundation

This skill operationalizes a practical assurance workflow informed by three complementary papers supplied during its design.

## 1. Transparent construction and validation of unstructured-data measures

Cong, Liang, Zhang, and Zhu, **“Textual Factors: A Scalable, Interpretable, and Data-Driven Approach to Analyzing Unstructured Information,”** NBER Working Paper 33168, 2024.

Key workflow implications:

- preserve an interpretable chain from text representation to clusters/factors and document loadings;
- combine data-driven methods with domain knowledge and human inspection;
- validate semantic coherence, historical/external sensibility, and out-of-sample performance;
- control hyperparameters and overfitting rather than choosing factors only by downstream significance;
- publish or preserve code so generated measures can be audited and reproduced.

Reference: https://www.nber.org/papers/w33168

## 2. Valid inference with generated covariates

Battaglia, Christensen, Hansen, and Sacher, **“Inference for Regression with Variables Generated by AI or Machine Learning,”** arXiv:2402.15585, version dated December 2024.

Key workflow implications:

- an AI/ML-generated covariate is a noisy estimate of a latent variable, not ordinary error-free data;
- ordinary two-step OLS can have first-order measurement-error bias even when conventional standard errors are consistent;
- confidence intervals may have plausible width but incorrect centering and under-cover the true parameter;
- the severity depends on measurement error relative to downstream sampling uncertainty;
- validated solutions include setting-specific bias correction and joint one-step estimation of measurement and outcome models;
- assumptions, identification, and the upstream model must be checked rather than treating any correction as universal.

Reference: https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.15585

## 3. Practical implementation of measurement-aware inference

Christensen and Hansen, **“Performing Valid Inference with AI/ML-Generated Covariates: A Guide for Empirical Practice.”**

Key workflow implications:

- for imputed binary labels, estimate false positives from a human-coded validation sample and propagate that uncertainty;
- for aggregated multiclass/index settings, use the classifier confusion matrix and the amount of source information per observation;
- compare naive two-step and bias-corrected results;
- preserve implementation code and notebooks rather than relying on prose descriptions;
- small misclassification probabilities can still produce materially different coefficients and intervals in large samples.

Software discussed by the authors: https://github.com/timothymchristensen/ValidMLInference

## Scope note

These papers support the measurement and inference portions of the skill. The data-lineage, deterministic-execution, logging, hashing, merge, attrition, and independent-reproduction controls are engineering and reproducibility safeguards added to make the workflow usable in real Stata projects. They do not by themselves prove causal identification or construct validity.
